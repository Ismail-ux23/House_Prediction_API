Hearthline uses the California Housing dataset's neighborhood signals to create a directional home value estimate. It is a fast lens, not an appraisal.

01

Enter the signals

Use the estimator for a single neighborhood profile. Income is entered in $10k units; location uses standard latitude and longitude.

The Random Forest model combines the eight inputs and compares their pattern with learned housing examples.

03

Read the range

The range is centered on the prediction and widened by the model's average absolute error, currently about $32,773.
